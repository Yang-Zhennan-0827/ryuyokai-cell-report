グラフを読むための3つの基礎

  • p16は「分裂のブレーキ」

    p16は、細胞が増えすぎないようにかけるブレーキで、がんを防ぐ仕組みの一つです。老化した細胞にも多く見られます。RNAが少ないことは、老化細胞が除かれた証明や安全性の証明ではありません。

  • 短い炎症と、続く炎症

    けがや感染の直後の炎症は、治すために必要な反応で、ふつうはおさまります。気になるのは、弱い炎症のサインが長く続く状態です。このページのRNAは、炎症の強さそのものではありません。

  • IDO1・RUNX2は、説明のあとで

    IDO1とRUNX2がどんな遺伝子かは、下の遺伝子の項で先に説明し、そのあとで培養条件ごとの値を示します（↓ 遺伝子の項へ）。犬の試料では補体などの遺伝子群のRNAが上がりました。どれもタンパク質や効果の測定ではなく、良い・悪いは判断できません。

Three basics for reading the chart

  • p16 is a “division brake”

    p16 is a brake that keeps cells from dividing too much — one of the body’s defences against cancer. Ageing cells often have more of it. Less RNA does not prove senescent cells were removed, or that anything is safe.

  • Short-lived vs lasting inflammation

    Inflammation right after an injury or infection is a needed repair response and usually settles. The concern is weak inflammatory signals that persist. The RNA on this page is not inflammation strength itself.

  • IDO1 and RUNX2: explained first, below

    What IDO1 and RUNX2 are is explained in the gene section below, before their values for each culture condition are shown (↓ gene section). In dog samples, complement and related gene-set RNA went up. None of these measure protein or an effect, so they are not “good” or “bad”.

Background research

University of Tokyo, Prof. Makoto Nakanishi et al. (2020)

Using p16 as a marker, the team examined senescent cells in mice one by one. p16-high cells increased with age and differed by organ. Removing these cells improved fat build-up and inflammation in a fatty-liver-inflammation model. What was removed were p16-high cells, not the p16 molecule. A mouse study separate from our culture comparison.

Methods & conditions

  • Samples: seven samples labelled donor L (one pre-culture sample and six culture conditions). Chart values are summarised over cells classified as MSC/stromal.
  • RNA detection: the share (%) of cells in which at least one RNA of the gene was found. Not protein amount or a senescent-cell count.
  • This is a gene-level CDKN2A RNA measurement; it does not directly measure p16-specific transcripts or the p16 protein.
  • Scores: exploratory values summarising RNA of a gene set against expression-matched reference genes (shown × 100, not %). Ranks follow the values stored in the source table.
  • Limits: a descriptive comparison between culture conditions; no statistical significance, treatment effect or before/after change in the same cells. Culture days, passage and serum also differ by condition.
